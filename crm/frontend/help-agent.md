# 👤 Agent Guide — RankBuilder CRM

Welcome, agent! This guide shows you how to work leads, log activity, generate quotes and understand your commissions.

## 1. Your Dashboard

When you log in you land on the **Dashboard** for your assigned client (e.g. House of Supreme).
It shows the lead pipeline funnel, your open leads, recent activity and rep productivity.

You see leads that are **assigned to you** or that **you created**. You work your own leads.

## 2. Working a Lead

Open the **Leads** tab. Each lead shows its source, status, location and assigned rep.
Click a lead to open the drawer with full details:

- **Contact info** — name, email, phone, suburb/location (editable; add the confirmed street address when you have it)
- **Source** — how the lead came in (website, HARO, call, WhatsApp, etc.)
- **Status** — NEW → REVIEWED → QUALIFIED → SENT → CONTACTED → CONVERTED / LOST
- **Type** — VALID / INVALID / FOLLOW_UP

### Kanban board
The **Kanban** view shows your leads as cards you can **drag between status columns**.
Dragging a card to CONVERTED or LOST also records the conversion outcome automatically —
no extra step needed.

### Follow-up logging
Every call or email you make should be logged. Open the lead, add a **follow-up** with:

- **Type** — Call / Email / WhatsApp / SMS / Note / Other
- **Outcome** — No answer / Left voicemail / Spoke to client / Sent (quote/docs) / Received reply / Other

Each attempt is stored as its own row so the full timeline is preserved — keep adding, never overwrite.

### Scheduled reminders
Use **Schedule Reminder** on a lead to get an email notification at a chosen date/time
(e.g. "call back Tuesday 09:00"). Pending reminders show on the lead; they're dismissed
automatically once the lead is converted, lost or archived.

### Documents
Attach files to a lead (quotes sent, client responses, signed docs) under the **Documents**
tab — pick a category (Email / Quote / Response / Other) so the trail stays organised.

## 3. Quotes

When a lead is ready, generate a quote from the lead record. The quote captures the
products, sizes and pricing agreed with the customer. Attach the sent quote to the lead
under Documents so there's a record.

## 4. AI Drafts

Use the AI draft helper to write a first-draft email or message to the lead.
Always review and personalise before sending — the AI draft is a starting point, not the final word.

## 5. Scoring

Keep VALID leads moving forward through the funnel; mark invalid ones quickly so you don't waste time.

## 6. Converting & Payment

When a deal is won:
1. **Move the lead to CONVERTED** (edit drawer or Kanban drag) — this records the conversion.
2. **Enter the correct amount** (quote / deal value) on the lead.
3. **Managers then mark Payment = Received** to action the deal into production.

Payment status shows as **Paid ✓** once received. A converted lead with payment received
feeds your commission record.

## 7. Commissions

Completed (CONVERTED) leads feed into your commission record. The pipeline and
rep-productivity views on the dashboard track your performance over time.

## Quick tips
- Log EVERY contact attempt — it builds the audit trail.
- Update status as the lead moves so the pipeline stays accurate.
- Tag source honestly (website, WhatsApp, call, etc.) — it drives reporting.
- Use the Export button to download your filtered lead list as CSV when needed.

Need help? Ask your client admin.
