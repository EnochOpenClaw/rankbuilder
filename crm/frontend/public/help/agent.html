Welcome, agent! This guide shows you how to work leads, log activity, generate quotes and understand your commissions.

1. Your Dashboard

When you log in you land on the Dashboard for your assigned client (e.g. House of Supreme). It shows the lead pipeline funnel, your open leads, recent activity and rep productivity.

You see leads that are assigned to you or that you created. You work your own leads.

2. Working a Lead

Open the Leads tab. Each lead shows its source, status, location and assigned rep. Click a lead to open the drawer with full details:

  • Contact info — name, email, phone, suburb/location
  • Source — how the lead came in (website, HARO, call, etc.)
  • Status — moves through the pipeline below
  • Type — VALID / INVALID / FOLLOW_UP

Lead pipeline

NEW→REVIEWED→ QUALIFIED→SENT→ CONTACTED→CONVERTED / LOST

Follow-up logging

Every call or email you make should be logged. Open the lead and add a follow-up note (e.g. "Called 08:00, no answer"). Each attempt is stored as its own row so the full timeline is preserved — keep adding, never overwrite.

3. Quotes

When a lead is ready, generate a quote from the lead record. The quote captures the products, sizes and pricing agreed with the customer.

4. AI Drafts

Use the AI draft helper to write a first-draft email or message to the lead.

Tip: Always review and personalise before sending — the AI draft is a starting point, not the final word.

5. Scoring

Keep VALID leads moving forward through the funnel; mark invalid ones quickly so you don't waste time.

6. Commissions

Completed (CONVERTED) leads feed into your commission record. The pipeline and rep-productivity views on the dashboard track your performance over time.

Quick Tips

Log EVERY contact attempt — it builds the audit trail.
Update status as the lead moves so the pipeline stays accurate.
Tag source honestly (website, WhatsApp, call, etc.) — it drives reporting.

Need help? Ask your client admin.
