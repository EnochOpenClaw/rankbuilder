## What Are Local Citations?

A local citation is any online mention of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP). Citations appear in:
- Business directories (Yellow Pages, Yell, etc.)
- Social media profiles
- Review sites
- Local chamber of commerce listings
- Industry-specific directories
- News sites and blogs

**Why they matter for SEO:**
- Google uses citations to confirm your business exists and is legitimate
- Consistent NAP across the web increases "prominence" signals
- Local citations are a confirmed ranking factor for local pack results
- The more quality citations, the higher you rank for "shutters Johannesburg" etc.

## Tools to Help Manage Citations

1. **Moz Local** — https://moz.com/local — Tracks citations, finds opportunities, monitors NAP consistency (paid)
2. **BrightLocal** — https://www.brightlocal.com — Citation building + tracking + review management (paid)
3. **Whitespark** — https://whitespark.ca — Citation finder + building service (paid)
4. **Yext** — https://www.yext.com — Enterprise-level citation management (expensive)
5. **Manual spreadsheet** — Free. Track every listing in a Google Sheet with URL, date added, login details, status.

**For a single-location SA business, manual is fine.** Use a spreadsheet with columns:
- Citation site
- URL of listing
- Date created
- Login email
- Status (live/pending/needs update)
- NAP matches? (Y/N)
