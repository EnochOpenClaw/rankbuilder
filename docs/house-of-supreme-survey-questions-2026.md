## PR Angle — Key Data Points to Emphasise

When publishing results, these are the numbers journalists will cite:

1. **Security priority ranking** vs. aesthetics — how does SA compare globally?
2. **% of SA homes with unprotected windows** (no shutters, bars, or security screens)
3. **Electricity spend vs. energy-saving behaviour** — link to Eskom tariff increases
4. **Burglary experience rate** — Q8 data, especially window-related
5. **Planned upgrade rate** — % planning to buy in next 12 months (the "market in waiting")
6. **Regional differences** — Gauteng vs. WC vs. KZN security priorities
7. **Avg. spend** — gives journalists a "R10k-R50k" figure to contextualise market size
