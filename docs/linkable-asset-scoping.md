## Why House of Supreme Needs a Linkable Asset

HOS already has decent blog content (measuring guides, care guides, "Why Custom Shutters Are Worth It"). 
But none of it is **citation-worthy** — something a journalist, blogger, or researcher would link to as a source.

- Original (not repackaged existing content)
- Data-driven or tool-based
- South Africa-specific

## Recommended: Option 1 — "South African Home Window Treatment Survey 2026"

### What It Is
Annual (or first-time) original research surveying 300-500 South African homeowners on:
- Window treatment types they use and why
- Top priorities: security, aesthetics, energy efficiency, privacy, noise
- Budget ranges for window upgrades
- Pain points with current solutions
- Awareness of shutter vs. blinds vs. curtains options
- Geographic differences (coastal vs. inland, urban vs. rural)
- Planned upgrades in next 12 months

### Why This Wins

**Link acquisition path:**
```
Publish survey → PR outreach to SA home improvement publications → 
Journalists cite data in articles → Link back to survey page as source
```

**Why it gets links organically:**
- Journalists writing about home improvement, energy efficiency, home security in SA need data. There's almost no SA-specific original research in this niche. This fills that gap.
- Renovation blogs, real estate sites (Property24, Private Property), home interest publications (Home Magazine SA, IdeaBox) will cite it.
- Real estate agents sharing on LinkedIn = amplification + links.

**Competitive moat:**
- No SA competitor has done this. Once HOS owns it, they own the citation.
- Repeatable annually — Year 2, Year 3 editions compound the data story.

**Dual purpose:**
- Powers RankBuilder outreach ("According to our 2026 SA Home Window Treatment Survey...")
- Directly markets HOS as the authority in SA's shutter market.

### What It Needs

**Survey distribution:**
- HOS customer email list (if 500+ subscribers)
- LinkedIn post from Craig's personal profile + Company Page
- South African home improvement Facebook groups
- WhatsApp community groups (if Craig has access)
- Organic: embedded on HOS website with form

**Target respondents:** 300 minimum for credible data. 500+ for PR release.

**Build:**
- Typeform / Google Forms (free) for survey collection
- Results page on HOS website (or subdirectory: houseofsupreme.co.za/survey-2026)
- PDF executive summary (downloadable, email-gated optional)
- Press release for SA publications

**Content outputs:**
1. **Full results page** — charts, key findings, methodology
2. **PR press release** — "New Survey Reveals SA Homeowners Prioritise X Over Y When It Comes to Window Treatments"
3. **LinkedIn article** — Craig's perspective on the findings
4. **HARO/Connectively pitch hook** — "SA data source available for journalists covering home improvement"

## Why Not the Calculator?

The ROI calculator (shutter cost vs. blinds over 5 years) is genuinely useful — but:
- Requires web development to build properly
- Harder to get journalists to cite a tool vs. a data point
- No real "story" for PR purposes
- Good to have as a secondary asset, not primary

---

## Recommendation

**Build the survey.** It's:
- High link potential with a clear acquisition path
- SA-specific original data (competitive moat)
- Works for both RankBuilder (earned media) and HOS (authority building)
- Not a technical build — no-code survey + HOS website results page
- Repeatable annually (compounding asset)

**This becomes RankBuilder's "original data" content strategy** — a feed of SA-specific home improvement surveys that journalists can cite, which generates permanent editorial links.
