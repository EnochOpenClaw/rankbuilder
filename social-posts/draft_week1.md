## POST 2 — Wednesday (Engagement/Question)
**Theme:** Industry observation  
**Platform:** LinkedIn + Facebook

### LinkedIn:
> 🪟 The flyscreen problem nobody talks about.
> 
> Every week we get calls from homeowners who had flyscreens fitted years ago and are now dealing with:
> - Mesh that's sagged or torn
> - Frames that no longer fit properly
> - A system that was clearly never designed for our conditions
> 
> The issue? Most flyscreen products sold in SA are imported from European climates. They weren't tested for:
> - UV intensity (our sun destroys standard mesh faster)
> - Temperature swings (materials expand/contract far more than European suppliers account for)
> - Coastal humidity vs. inland heat (totally different failure modes)
> 
> We've spent years developing build-ups that actually work for South African conditions. Not the easy route — but the right one.
> 
> What's the biggest home improvement mistake you've made? Learned the hard way counts double.
